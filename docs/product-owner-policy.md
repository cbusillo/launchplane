---
title: Retired Client Policy Records
---

Client membership, requirement, and routing policies are retired under
[DIRECTION.md](../DIRECTION.md). Their evaluators and `/v1/product-owner/*`
administration/read routes have been deleted. Existing payload contracts,
tables, migrations, and storage history remain readable; they do not determine
current approval requirements.

The product profile's immutable Client GitHub identity controls current
[Client review](owner-acceptance.md) and [release review](release-review.md).
The supported product-profile Client setting operation remains available under
its existing authorization. This retirement changes no Client identity, grant,
record, or production deployment.
